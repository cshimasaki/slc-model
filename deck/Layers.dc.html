03  ·  Why you, and not a lender

Grant capital is the only layer that can absorb a ramp

MORTAL CAPITAL
The Tontine

Pensioners buy a lifetime annuity. We pay 4.75% while they live; on death the charge is extinguished and no principal is ever repaid.

Expensive per year. It ends.
PERMANENT CAPITAL
Community shares

Local people take a capped, discretionary 5%. It never matures, and neither does the interest on it.

Cheaper per year. It never ends.
FREE CAPITAL
Grants and gifts

Asks nothing back, so it charges nothing in the years when there is nothing to charge it against.

The only layer that survives year one.
A patient loan at 5% does not help a ramp. It starts charging on day one, while the rent is still arriving — it makes the J-curve deeper, not shallower.
