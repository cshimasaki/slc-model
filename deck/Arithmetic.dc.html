Rent will only ever pay for half a house

A three-bedroom terrace in Stroud costs £302,300 all-in and lets for £1,176 a month.

After maintenance, voids and a sinking fund, what is left services about £158,000 of interest-bearing capital at a cover ratio that survives a severe downturn.

The remaining £144,000 a house has to be capital that asks for nothing back.

This is not a financing gap to be closed with cleverness. It is the arithmetic of charging less than the market.

Every lever we tested moves this a little and none of it away — lower gearing, cheaper stock, higher yields, scale efficiency. Below-market rent and fully-priced capital do not meet.
