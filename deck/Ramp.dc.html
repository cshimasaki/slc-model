02  ·  Where it breaks

Strong over fifty years. Cannot survive its own beginning.

Fixed costs and interest arrive in year one. Rent arrives as houses do. The surplus per house is negative until roughly year ten — and then never looks back.

And growing faster makes it worse

Buying in blocks rather than one house at a time reaches 230 homes by year 15 instead of 55. Cover in the ramp years falls to 0.84× — while cover from year 20 onward sits above 4×.

The failure is a J-curve, not insolvency. The assets are sound; the first decade is not funded.
